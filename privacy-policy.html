Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your information

Last updated: June 2026

At Funboot, your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how we keep it safe. By using Funboot, you agree to this policy.

1. Who We Are

Funboot is an activity and event discovery app that helps users find things to do near them. Our contact email is funboot.support@gmail.com.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information you provide

  • Account information — your email address and full name when you sign up
  • Profile information — your base city if you choose to add it
  • User-created events — event titles, descriptions, venue names, links and dates you submit for moderation

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Location data — your GPS coordinates when you tap "Use my location" or verify attendance at a place or event. We only access location when you explicitly request it.
  • Activity data — places you mark as visited, events you attend, favourites you save, and Funboot points you earn
  • Device information — device type and operating system version for app performance purposes

2.3 Information from third parties

  • Google Places API — activity names, addresses and location data for nearby suggestions
  • PredictHQ — event data including names, dates and venues for events near you
  • SeatGeek — event listings and ticketing links for US, UK and Canada events
  • Google Geocoding API — converts your GPS coordinates to a readable city name

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide activity and event recommendations near your location
  • To verify your attendance at places and events using GPS comparison
  • To track your Funboot points, adventure count and level progression
  • To send push notifications for events you have marked attendance on
  • To moderate user-submitted events before they appear publicly
  • To improve the app's accuracy and recommendation quality

4. Location Data

Funboot requests your location only in the foreground — we never track your location in the background or when the app is closed. Location is used exclusively to:

  • Find activities and events near you
  • Verify you are physically present at a place or event when you claim attendance

We do not sell your location data to advertisers or third parties.

5. Push Notifications

If you mark attendance on an event, we schedule local push notifications to remind you at event start time and 30 minutes into the event. These notifications are scheduled on your device and do not require a server connection once set. You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings.

6. Data Storage and Security

Your account data and activity history are stored securely on Supabase — a cloud database provider with enterprise-grade encryption at rest and in transit. We use Row Level Security (RLS) to ensure each user can only access their own data.

We never store your raw GPS coordinates permanently — location is used in-memory for the duration of the verification check and is not logged to our database.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with:

  • Supabase — for secure data storage
  • Google — for Maps, Places and Geocoding APIs (subject to Google's Privacy Policy)
  • PredictHQ and SeatGeek — event data APIs (your location coordinates are sent to retrieve nearby events)

All third-party services we use are bound by their own privacy policies and applicable data protection laws.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Delete your account and all associated data — use the "Deactivate Account" option in the Profile screen or email us
  • Correct inaccurate data — update your name and city in the Profile screen
  • Opt out of push notifications — via your device's notification settings

9. Children's Privacy

Funboot is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately at funboot.support@gmail.com.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of Funboot after changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact Us

For any privacy-related questions or requests, please contact us at:
funboot.support@gmail.com
